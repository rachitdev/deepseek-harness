# Glossary — Google Cloud AI Reports (ROI of AI in CX 2025; AI Agent Trends 2026)

Source key: **R1** = The ROI of AI in Customer Experience (Google Cloud + National Research Group, fieldwork Apr 18 – Jun 3, 2025). **R2** = Google Cloud AI Agent Trends 2026.

**A2A (Agent2Agent) protocol** — Open standard enabling integration and orchestration between AI agents built by different developers, on different frameworks, or owned by different organizations (R2).

**Agent accelerator** — In the five-pillar learning model, the technical expert responsible for turning prioritized employee ideas into functional AI solutions (R2).

**Agent orchestrator** — Emerging role, also called "Chief of Staff for AI," for the person who directs a system of agents. R2 states the expertise "simply doesn't exist in the market yet."

**Agent supervisor (human supervisor of agents)** — R2's model in which every employee's primary job becomes orchestrating specialized agents rather than performing tasks personally.

**Agentic AI** — AI that moves beyond answering questions to understanding a goal, making a plan, and taking actions across applications to achieve it, with extensive human guidance and oversight (R2).

**Agentic AI early adopter** — R1's defined cohort: organizations allocating at least 50% of future AI budget to AI agents and already running agents deeply embedded across operations. Survey base n=460.

**Agentic concierge** — A customer-facing agent grounded in enterprise context (CRM history, logistics data) that recognizes the customer and their situation without identification prompts (R2).

**Agentic ecommerce** — Commerce in which a non-human entity executes the purchase decision under human pre-approval, requiring new authority and accountability frameworks (R2).

**Agentic search** — Natural-language discovery in which agents comprehend complex questions and intent rather than matching keywords, delivering personalized results and recommendations (R1).

**Agentic SOC** — A security operations center orchestrating a system of task-based AI agents around a shared security outcome, with recommendation and escalation remaining human-managed (R2).

**Agentic system** — "A digital assembly line — a human-guided, multi-step workflow that orchestrates multiple agents to run a business process end to end" (R2).

**AgentIQ** — Mr. Cooper's internal tool, incorporating Google AI, credited with a 3.53% average agent handle time improvement across 500,000 monthly calls (R1).

**AHT (average agent handle time)** — Contact center metric; the Mr. Cooper case reports a 3.53% improvement yielding 28,000 hours annually (R1).

**AI agent (survey definition)** — R1's exact wording, which governs every adoption statistic: "specialized LLMs that have specific roles, context, and objectives to independently plan, reason, and perform tasks with access to data function call APIs and can interact with other AI agents if needed. These can be pre-built or in-house built agents."

**AI rulebook** — R1's checklist item: enterprise-wide guidelines to secure data, protect IP, and ensure compliance, established before scaling rather than after.

**Alert fatigue** — Analyst degradation from alert and data volume; 82% of security professionals are concerned or very concerned they may be missing real threats because of it (Forrester Consulting for Google, July 2025, cited in R2).

**Analyst agent** — In R2's marketing example, the agent monitoring market trends, competitor announcements, and social sentiment 24/7 and delivering a daily one-page report.

**AP2 (Agent Payments Protocol)** — Google framework for agent-initiated payments under human pre-approval, addressing proof of user authority, hallucination risk in merchant requests, and fraud accountability (R2).

**Bene** — Commerzbank's specialized chatbot built on Customer Engagement Suite; handles over 2 million chats and resolves 70% of all inquiries (R1).

**BigQuery** — Google Cloud data platform named as an MCP-connectable source and used in the Suzano case (R2).

**Beyond AI Optimism** — Google, 2025 research cited in R2 for the employee-side statistics (61% daily AI use, 84% want more focus, 29% say AI is broadly advocated).

**CodeMender** — Google DeepMind AI agent that automatically improves code security; early results include finding new zero-day vulnerabilities in well-tested software (R2).

**Content agent** — In R2's marketing example, drafts social copy and blog articles in the company's brand voice from a weekly strategic theme, for human review.

**Creative agent** — In R2's marketing example, generates images and video from the marketing strategy, creative guidelines, and social copy.

**C-suite sponsorship (comprehensive)** — R1's rating-5 definition: "Comprehensive C-level sponsorship and clear corporate vision for gen AI objectives." 78% of this group (n=1,269) report ROI now on at least one gen AI use case.

**Customer Engagement Suite** — Google Cloud's CX product line, used in the Commerzbank and Verizon cases; the subject of the Forrester TEI study reporting 207% three-year ROI (R1).

**Data agent** — In R2's marketing example, sifts millions of structured and unstructured data points for actionable market patterns.

**Digital assembly line** — R2's metaphor for a multi-agent, human-guided end-to-end business process.

**Digital hub** — Pillar 03 of R2's learning model: an interactive platform with a gamified idea exchange and leaderboard for collecting and rewarding AI use cases.

**Executive sponsor** — In the three-role sponsorship team, provides funding, high-level backing, and consistent messaging on AI's importance (R2).

**Field Days** — Structured, challenge-based events (in person or virtual) where teams practice using new custom AI tools (R2, pillar 04).

**Gemini Enterprise** — Google Cloud offering that lets employees build and manage their own specialized AI agents (R2).

**Go Autonomous** — Partner whose AI agents on Google Cloud automate Danfoss's email-based order processing (R2).

**Grounding** — "The process of anchoring an AI model's responses to a specific, verifiable set of facts — its 'ground truth.' For an enterprise, this ground truth is its own internal data" (R2).

**Groundswell lead** — The "AI megaphone" in the three-role sponsorship team: grassroots campaigns, excitement, and collecting employee ideas (R2).

**Human plus machine** — R1's named deployment strategy producing AI-augmented "super agents" that guide pre-purchase, empower reps post-purchase, and turn the contact center into a problem-solving hub.

**Human-in-the-loop** — R1 checklist item and R2 design constraint; humans remain orchestrators and final decision-makers.

**Instruction-based computing** — The prior model where the human specifies the operation (analyze this spreadsheet, develop this code) (R2).

**Intent-based computing** — R2's 2026 shift: the employee states a desired outcome and the LLM-plus-agent system determines how to deliver it.

**Levels of AI agent maturity** — R1's three-level ladder: Level 1 simple tasks (chatbots, information retrieval, image generation); Level 2 AI agent applications (customer service agents, creative agents); Level 3 multi-agent workflows (agentic workflows, agent orchestration).

**Magic Apron** — Home Depot's AI agent offering 24/7 how-to instructions, product recommendations, and review summaries (R2).

**MCP (Model Context Protocol)** — Standardized two-way connection allowing LLM applications to connect to data sources and tools; solves frozen training-time knowledge and inability to act externally (R2).

**MEA aggregate** — R1's reporting grouping for small-sample markets: UAE (40), Turkey (35), Saudi Arabia (18), South Africa (7), Qatar (7), Kuwait (3).

**National Research Group (NRG)** — The research partner that fielded R1's survey with Google Cloud.

**Net new budget** — Funding gen AI without reducing other budgets: 58% in 2025, down from 61% in 2024 (R1).

**NotebookLM** — Google tool used both to analyze R2's inputs and to power its "Ask the report" interactive version.

**NPS (Net Promoter Score)** — 75% of executives reporting meaningful gen AI impact on CX also report improved user satisfaction or NPS (R1, n=2,177).

**Reallocation (non-AI budget)** — Funding gen AI by moving money from non-AI budgets: 48% in 2025, up from 44% in 2024 (R1).

**Reporting agent** — In R2's marketing example, connects to analytics platforms and delivers a Friday one-page campaign summary.

**SAIF 2.0 (Secure AI Framework)** — Google's expanded framework addressing risks posed by autonomous AI agents (R2).

**Sauter** — Partner working with Suzano and Google Cloud on the natural-language-to-SQL agent (R2).

**Smart handoff** — The concierge agent's escalation move: transferring a complex or emotionally charged case to human staff with a full summary (R2).

**SOAR (security orchestration, automation and remediation)** — Prior-generation security automation which R2 says "may offer only incremental benefits" because it cannot reason, observe, and adjust.

**SOC (security operations center)** — The environment R2's trend 4 addresses.

**Socrates** — Torq's AI SOC analyst coordinating specialized agents; 90% of tier-1 tasks auto-remediated, 95% decrease in manual tasks, 10x faster response (R2).

**Super agent** — An AI-augmented human customer service representative under the human-plus-machine strategy (R1).

**TEI (Total Economic Impact)** — Forrester's commissioned study methodology; the 2025 TEI of Google Cloud Customer Engagement Suite with Google AI reports 207% ROI over three years with payback under six months (cited in R1).

**Vertex AI** — Google Cloud's AI platform, linked from R2's agentic ecommerce section.

**Vertex AI Search for Commerce** — Product used by Toolstation, credited with a 5.5% increase in search-based revenue and a 10% lift in click-through rates (R1).
